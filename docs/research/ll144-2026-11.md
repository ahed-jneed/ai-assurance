## Legal-review items for #57

Each item is pending. The platform's current reading is stated; a lawyer's answer may change the pack, the computation or the summary.

- **LR-LL144-01 Currency of the law.** No amendment of Administrative Code §§ 20-870 to 20-874 or 6 RCNY §§ 5-300 to 5-304 after 5 July 2023 was found on the City's rules site. Bills before the City Council and any State legislation were not researched. Confirm that the 2023 text is current.
- **LR-LL144-02 Statute not read.** The Administrative Code text (definitions of AEDT and bias audit in § 20-870, the duties in § 20-871, penalties in § 20-872) was not read from an official source; framework entries LL144-20-871-A and LL144-20-871-B are marked unverified. Confirm the duties and that the rules are the complete computational requirement.
- **LR-LL144-03 Full-sample median.** § 5-301(c)(1) requires the median for "the full sample of applicants"; the platform includes individuals in an unknown category in the median and excludes them only from per-category rates (section 10.2, Q4).
- **LR-LL144-04 "Above" the median.** The scoring-rate definition counts scores above the sample's median; the platform counts strictly above, so scores equal to the median do not count. With many ties this changes rates materially.
- **LR-LL144-05 The 2% base and the reference category.** The platform measures "less than 2% of the data being used for the bias audit" against all assessed individuals, unknown category included, and takes the impact-ratio reference among the categories not excluded. Confirm both readings.
- **LR-LL144-06 Categories.** The platform uses the EEO-1 Component 1 categories (two sex categories, seven race/ethnicity categories) and the intersection of sex with race/ethnicity, as in the DCWP examples. Values outside those categories (for example a non-binary sex) are counted as unknown only if the auditor maps them so; the platform warns but does not decide. Confirm how such individuals must be reported.
- **LR-LL144-07 Data reliance and sufficiency.** The platform records the auditor's attestation on § 5-302 but decides neither whether an employer may rely on other employers' historical data nor what makes historical data insufficient "for a statistically significant bias audit"; the rules do not define the test.
- **LR-LL144-08 Audit date and the summary's form.** The platform takes the audit date the auditor states (no later than the computation date) as "the date of the most recent bias audit" and computes the one-year limit from it; it does not decide whether the date of the data, the report or the sign-off counts. It also does not decide whether the summary page meets "clear and conspicuous" posting, which is the employer's duty.

## Not in scope

The platform does not assess compliance with anti-discrimination law (federal, State or the NYC Human Rights Law), does not apply a four-fifths or any other threshold to LL144 figures (the rules set none; section 10.2), and does not check the employer's posting, notices or retention beyond what the optional working paper LL144-FW-04 inspects.
